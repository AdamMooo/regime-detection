The verdict in one picture — the fee against its null bands

The FKO fee (γ=10) measures what a risk-averse investor would pay to hold the strategy instead of the benchmark. The point sits inside every null band, and vs vol targeting the fee is significantly negative. The fee is exposure, not timing.
