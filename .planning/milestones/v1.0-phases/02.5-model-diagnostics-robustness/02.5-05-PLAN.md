**Section 6: Limitations & Caveats**

6.1 **Model Limitations**
- Markov assumption: Assumes regime depends only on current state, not long-term history
  - Implication: May misclassify slow-moving regime shifts (e.g., decade-long bull market)
- Regime persistence: Assumes average regime dwell 10–60 days
  - Implication: May underestimate regime fragility during crisis
- Feature engineering: Uses 13 hand-picked features
  - Implication: May miss novel risk factors (e.g., geopolitical shocks, cyber attacks)

6.2 **GARCH Limitations**
- Volatility mean-reversion: Assumes volatility reverts to long-run mean
  - Implication: Fails during regime shifts (lag in tail risk estimation)
- Parameter estimation: GARCH fitted on historical data; uncertainty not quantified
  - Implication: Parameters may be stale (recalibration needed quarterly)
- Small-sample bias: Requires ≥30 observations per regime
  - Implication: Early backtesting windows may fail GARCH fitting

6.3 **Operational Limitations**
- Forward-pass only: Regime labels non-causal in backtesting
  - Implication: Live trading uses forward-pass labels (causal); backtest uses filtered labels (non-causal)
  - Workaround: Walk-forward validation bridge gap; signals match live behavior
- Incremental training: Each data update retrains HMM
  - Implication: Regime labels may shift slightly on new data
  - Workaround: Hysteresis (5+ day hold) suppresses label noise
