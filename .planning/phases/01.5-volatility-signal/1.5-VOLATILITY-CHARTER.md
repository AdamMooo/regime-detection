# Volatility Signal — Research Charter

**Charter v1.0** — filled against framework template **v1.1** (`research-charter-template.md`).

This is a **pre-registration** artifact for the **reframed** volatility signal — continuous descriptors, **no
state**. It supersedes the inherited jump-model instrument (retired; retro-fit declaration at
`.planning/framework/examples/volatility-signal-declaration.md` is that instrument's record, not this signal). It
answers the six charter questions in order (mapping to spec attributes 1 / 2 / 3-context / 5 / 6 / 8) and declares,
in advance, both what would make the signal **earn admission** and the specific results that would **REJECT** it.

**Objective:** determine whether the reframed volatility signal *earns admission under the framework* as a
descriptive context barometer — NOT to prove volatility "predicts" anything (it makes no forward-return claim).

**Descriptive, not predictive — read this first.** Every other charter (valuation, credit, …) validates a
*forecastability* claim. Volatility does not. Its content is a **measurement of a proven stylized fact**
(volatility clustering / persistence — Mandelbrot 1963; Engle 1982 ARCH; Bollerslev 1986 GARCH), presented as a
continuous barometer. Consequently the validation bar (Q4) is **measurement correctness + international
replication of the descriptive properties**, and falsification (Q5) targets the *measurement being an artifact*,
never a missed return forecast. This shape was decided with Adam (2026-08-05) and is load-bearing for the whole
document.

Any later positive / SUPPORT claim still requires one-look + the frozen protocol + overnight cooling-off + Adam's
dated sign-off (`validation-standards.md` §(e)). Deviations from the analysis spec below are disclosed with a
dated note, never silently edited (registered-report discipline).

## Stage-0 Relevance Gate (PASSED)

The gate is the pre-charter triage, default = NO. Volatility clears all five overwhelmingly — it is the
foundational axis of the observatory.

- **G1 — investment question:** how large is current market volatility, how unusual is that level versus history,
  which way is it moving, and how durable is the current shock?
- **G2 — why it matters to long-term investors:** volatility is the single most important *context* conditioner of
  the whole observatory — it changes how every other reading should be interpreted, and it is the risk-management
  channel (the environment in which drawdowns are borne). It is the most robust quantity in all of empirical
  finance.
- **G3 — unique information:** the fast, coincident realized-risk axis (PC1) read from return dynamics. No other
  admitted signal carries current realized volatility level / persistence; the inflation/real-rate correlation
  axis is the slow PC2, mechanistically orthogonal.
- **G4 — leave-one-out:** removing volatility leaves the observatory blind to the current risk environment and
  strips every other signal of its context. The single largest information loss of any signal ⇒ keep.
- **G5 — ex-ante prior:** overwhelming. Volatility clustering / persistence is the most replicated stylized fact
  in asset pricing (Mandelbrot 1963; Engle 1982; Bollerslev 1986), universal across markets and eras.

## The six charter questions

### 1. What question does the signal answer?

Volatility answers **four descriptive questions about the current market environment**, as a continuous barometer
with **no state label**:

1. **How high?** — the level of annualised volatility right now, and its **percentile** against its own history
   (causal expanding window).
2. **Which way?** — the short-horizon **drift** of that percentile (is volatility rising, cooling, or holding).
3. **How unusual?** — **rarity** on two lenses: the full-century distribution (long memory) and the trailing-year
   fraction that ran hotter (recent context).
4. **How durable is the current shock?** — the **persistence / half-life** of the volatility process: how long an
   innovation to volatility takes to decay. *(This descriptor is registered here and built next; the other three
   exist in `scripts/vol_descriptors.py` + `scripts/vol_read.py`.)*

Unique information (feeds the investment-usefulness axis): the fast realized-risk axis (PC1) that no other admitted
signal contains, and that all of them are read *against*. A market can be cheap and calm, or cheap and violently
volatile — volatility is the axis that distinguishes those environments.

### 2. What market assumption does it monitor?

**None — N/A, because volatility is a context axis, not an assumption-monitor.** Unlike stock-bond correlation
("bonds hedge equity drawdowns") or valuation ("equities are priced for near-normal long-horizon returns"),
volatility does not assert a structural assumption that can be intact or violated. It is the barometer against
which the *other* assumptions are read. There is deliberately no {intact | under-test | violated} status field —
introducing one would re-import the CALM/STRESSED state the reframe exists to remove. The ledger carries
volatility as a **reading** (level · percentile · drift · rarity · persistence), never a status.

### 3. What mechanism supports it — why does it survive being known?

**Registered mechanism: risk-management channel via volatility clustering / persistence.**

**The stylized fact.** Volatility is autocorrelated and mean-reverting: large moves cluster, calm clusters, and
shocks decay over a finite half-life. This is the ARCH/GARCH lineage (Engle 1982; Bollerslev 1986) and the oldest
observation in the literature (Mandelbrot 1963). It is not a theory to be discovered here; it is a measured,
universal property, which is exactly why the mechanism gate is passed trivially and the honest work is
*measurement quality*, not existence-of-effect.

**Why it survives being known.** The signal makes **no tradeable edge claim**, so there is nothing for public
knowledge to arbitrage away. It reports *what the current volatility environment is and how persistent it tends to
be* — a descriptive fact about the process, not a mispricing. Persistence is precisely what makes today's reading
informative about the near-term environment (not about returns): because volatility is autocorrelated, today's
level and the estimated shock half-life carry information about the environment tomorrow. Knowing this changes
nothing about the fact.

**Not a risk-premium channel.** Volatility here is the *state of the risk environment*, not a harvested premium
(the volatility-risk-premium is a separate, killed idea in this repo — see the record; it is not revived here).

Maps to spec attribute 2; gated by `validation-standards.md` §(b) — passed (a written, structural, universal
reason).

### 4. What evidence would validate it?

Because the signal is descriptive, validation is **measurement correctness + international replication of the
descriptive properties** — never forecastability. Each item is paired with its reject condition in Q5.

- **V1 — measurement is causal and look-ahead-free.** Every descriptor uses only data through *t*: EWMA variance
  recurrence (causal), expanding-window percentile (not full-sample rank), trailing-window persistence fit. A
  code + construction review confirms no forward filtering and no final-revision leak. *(Built from returns only →
  no macro vintage surface; stated explicitly per §(a).)*
- **V2 — estimator robustness.** The qualitative reading (level band, percentile, rarity, persistence direction)
  does **not flip** under reasonable estimator choices: EWMA decay λ ∈ {0.94, 0.97} and a range-based estimator
  (Parkinson / Garman–Klass from the OHLC panels) alongside close-to-close. The barometer is a property of the
  market, not of a tuning knob.
- **V3 — persistence is real and estimable.** The volatility process shows material positive persistence and a
  finite, sensibly-scaled shock half-life, estimated causally (see analysis spec). "How durable is the shock" must
  be an actual, stable number, not noise.
- **V4 — rarity calibration is well-behaved.** The expanding percentile and the trailing-year rarity read are
  monotone and non-degenerate (not dominated by a single early-sample regime; not internally contradictory in a
  way that misleads). The century distribution used for "how unusual" is not an artifact of one crisis.
- **V5 — out-of-hypothesis-sample replication (closes RF).** The descriptive properties — clustering, a
  same-order-of-magnitude shock half-life, and sensible level/percentile/rarity behavior — **replicate on the
  Japan and Europe panels** under identical construction, or a documented failure is recorded. This is the
  standing D-(d) requirement and the concrete run that **closes the RF open item** (the reframed signal gets the
  literal panel run the jump-model tag rested a stylized-fact clause on).

Maps to spec attribute 5.

### 5. What would falsify it?

Declared before any look. Falsification targets the **measurement**, not a missed forecast (a descriptive signal
of a universal fact cannot "fail to predict"; it can only be a bad measurement). Each reject condition is paired
with the validation test it trips.

- **R1 — look-ahead leak.** Any descriptor found to use future data (full-sample rank, final-revision inputs,
  non-causal smoothing). Fatal; the reading would be unusable live. (Trips V1.)
- **R2 — estimator artifact.** The qualitative reading flips under reasonable λ or between close-to-close and
  range-based estimators — i.e. the barometer is an artifact of the tuning knob, not a market property. (Trips V2.)
- **R3 — persistence not estimable.** The shock half-life is unstable / not identifiable / economically
  implausible (e.g. negative or century-scale), so "how durable is the current shock" cannot be answered honestly.
  (Trips V3.)
- **R4 — rarity read misleads.** The percentile is degenerate or unstable in the early sample, or the trailing-year
  and century lenses contradict each other in a way that would mislead a reader rather than inform. (Trips V4.)
- **R5 — OOS failure.** The descriptive properties do not replicate on Japan / Europe (clustering absent, half-life
  a different order of magnitude, or rarity behavior nonsensical). Would mean the construction is US-specific, not
  a universal measurement. (Trips V5.)

Note on the burden: R1–R5 are all about *whether the measurement is honest and robust*. There is deliberately no
"volatility failed to predict returns" reject condition — the signal never claims to.

Maps to spec attribute 6 (echoed as user-facing caveats in attribute 8).

### 6. What does it explicitly NOT claim?

The per-signal boundary. Volatility reports its four-part reading (level · percentile · drift · rarity ·
persistence) and **STOPS**. It implies no action.

Explicit prohibitions (this list is a boundary statement; the forbidden vocabulary below appears only to prohibit
it): the signal is **NOT** a CALM/STRESSED state or any regime label; **NOT** a switch, trigger, or timing signal;
**NOT** a forecast of *when* volatility will rise or fall; **NOT** a crash / bear-market predictor (it is
coincident, and historically a level read misses fast crashes with no volatility build-up); **NOT** a return
forecast; and never a buy / sell / risk-on / risk-off statement, an exposure or allocation instruction, or any
single-number market-safety score. It is a **barometer, not a switch**: it describes the current environment and
its persistence, never what to do about it. Investment decisions live in a separate system and with the human;
this signal only reduces a blind spot in that human's judgment.

Maps to spec attribute 8.

## Pre-registered analysis specification (frozen before the look)

Operationalizes V1–V5 / R1–R5. Any deviation is logged with a dated note. Choices previously marked
`[recommended — Adam to confirm/override]` were **confirmed by Adam 2026-08-05** (both the estimator-robustness
panel and the AR(1)-log-vol half-life); they are now frozen registered choices.

- **Level estimator (primary):** EWMA variance recurrence `σ²_t = λ σ²_{t-1} + (1−λ) r²_t`, λ = 0.94
  (RiskMetrics daily), annualised by √252, 252-day burn-in seeded on year-1 sample variance; causal.
  (`scripts/vol_descriptors.py:ewma_vol`.)
- **Estimator robustness panel [V2] (confirmed 2026-08-05):** λ = 0.97 (slower decay) + a **range-based** estimator
  (Parkinson high-low and/or Garman–Klass) built from the OHLC panels (`data/processed/ohlc_{spy,nikkei,stoxx}.csv`).
  Declared before the look so admission cannot rest on "whichever λ read best."
- **Percentile / rarity [V4]:** causal **expanding-window** rank in [0,1] (`vol_descriptors.py:expanding_percentile`);
  two rarity lenses reported — full-history expanding percentile (long memory) + trailing-252d fraction-hotter
  (recent context). Both already in `scripts/vol_read.py`.
- **Drift:** change in the level percentile over the trailing ~10 sessions (rising / cooling / holding). Already in
  `vol_read.py`.
- **Shock persistence / half-life [V3]:** report the **half-life = days for a volatility shock to half-decay**,
  as the "how durable is the current shock" answer.
  - *Originally registered (confirmed 2026-08-05):* AR(1) on log realized volatility,
    `log σ_t = c + φ log σ_{t-1} + ε_t`, half-life `= ln(2)/(−ln φ)`, causal trailing-window fit.
  - **DEVIATION (2026-08-05, disclosed per §(e).5 — Adam-directed "do this right"):** replaced by
    **GARCH(1,1)-t persistence**, `σ²_t = ω + α ε²_{t-1} + β σ²_{t-1}`, half-life `= ln(0.5)/ln(α+β)`, fit on a
    **trailing ~5yr window refit quarterly** (`scripts/vol_descriptors.py:shock_half_life`). *Why the AR(1) was
    rejected in construction:* fitting AR(1) to a rolling-window realized-vol series manufactures autocorrelation
    from the shared-return overlap (lag-1 φ → (m−1)/m by construction), so its half-life tracked the smoothing
    window, not the market — a V2 estimator-artifact failure caught before any positive claim. GARCH models the
    latent conditional variance directly from returns with no smoothing-window artifact, and α+β is the canonical
    persistence parameter. *Why trailing not expanding:* a century-long fit mixes volatility regimes and inflates
    α+β toward 1 (near-IGARCH, spurious persistence; Lamoureux–Lastrapes 1990); a trailing window reads the
    current regime's persistence, which is genuinely regime-conditional. Guard: half-life NaN when α+β ≥ 1.
  - **ROLLING-ONLY, locked 2026-08-05.** We considered carrying BOTH a rolling and an expanding GARCH. Decided
    against: an OOS variance-forecast horse race (US, QLIKE, `scratchpad/garch_horserace.py`) found rolling vs
    expanding a statistical dead heat (DM t = 0.65), so rolling costs no forecast skill; and the expanding
    persistence is a near-constant near-IGARCH value that fails the D-19 leave-one-out bar as a live reading. So
    ONE live descriptor (rolling); "how unusual is today's durability" comes from the **expanding percentile of
    the rolling half-life against its own history** (`half_life_pctile`), not a parallel expanding fit; the
    structural near-IGARCH persistence stays a documented stylized fact (mechanism, attribute 3).
  - **Registered v-next upgrade:** component / spline-GARCH (Engle–Rangel) to split short-run shock persistence
    from the slow-drifting baseline level — the principled way to serve both timescales in one model, and the
    purist decontamination of the regime-mixing problem. Shipped as a later descriptor version (semver §(g));
    version A (rolling-window GARCH) is v1.0.
- **Out-of-hypothesis-sample [V5]:** Japan (`data/processed/japan_market_daily.csv`, `ohlc_nikkei.csv`) + Europe
  (`europe_market_daily.csv`, `ohlc_stoxx.csv`), identical construction, before any SUPPORT claim. Run through the
  generic `run_oos` harness (D-20) built in task 1.5-01, so the same causal primitives serve every later signal.
- **Point-in-time discipline:** built purely from asset returns / OHLC → **no macro vintage or revision surface**
  (stated explicitly per §(a)). The only live-data concern is the estimator warm-up (burn-in reported as NaN).
