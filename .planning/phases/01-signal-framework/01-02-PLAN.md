<objective>
Specify the multidimensional research-maturity confidence model, the descriptive maturity model, and the
historical-context output / assumption-ledger shape (Level 0/1/2); then retro-fit the whole frozen framework
against the two already-built signals to prove it fits heterogeneous shapes before freeze, and compile the
sign-off package for the items that require Adam's dated approval. Documentation/specification only — no code, no
signal build.

Purpose: Give every future signal one place to render (a known output shape, never a score), make confidence a
reproducible statement of research maturity (never a likelihood), and prove the template is real by filling it
against volatility and stock-bond correlation before it is frozen at v1.0.
Output: `.planning/framework/signal-output-spec.md`, two example declarations under
`.planning/framework/examples/`, and `.planning/framework/SIGNOFF-CHECKLIST.md`.

Decisions covered: D-06 (research-maturity confidence, four revised dimensions); D-13 (maturity descriptive not a
ranking); D-14 (Level 0/1/2 names + STOP at Level 2); D-08, D-09 (output/ledger shape); D-02, D-03 (invariants);
D-11 (no-dominance / co-equal presentation).
</objective>
